## 1. Introduction & Overview

At **Remaini** ("we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, storage, and handling of information when you use the Remaini mobile application (the "App") across all supported platforms.

**Remaini is designed from the ground up as an offline-first application with zero tracking, zero personal data harvesting, and no remote user profiling.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe that your personal events, milestones, and countdown memories belong strictly to you. Therefore:

* **No Account Registration:** You do not need to create an account, register, or log in with an email or phone number to use Remaini.
* **No Personal Data Harvesting:** We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, physical location, contacts, calendar entries, photos, or device identifiers.
* **No Analytics or Telemetry:** We do not embed third-party analytics trackers, crash-reporting SDKs, or behavioral profiling tools (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Facebook Pixel).
* **No Advertising Networks:** Remaini is 100% ad-free. We do not integrate third-party ad networks or display targeted advertising.
* **No Cloud Event Syncing:** Your countdown events, memos, custom dates, categories, and color choices are never transmitted to or stored on external servers or cloud databases.

---

## 3. How Your Data is Stored (Local On-Device Storage)

* All data you create in the App (including countdown titles, target dates, categories, color preferences, memos, pinned statuses, and app settings) is stored **locally on your device** using high-performance, encrypted on-device database storage (Hive CE).
* We have zero access to your stored countdown data.
* If you delete an event or tap **"Clear All Data"** in the Settings screen, that data is permanently deleted from your device storage immediately.

---

## 4. Device Permissions & System Interactions

Remaini requests minimal system capabilities necessary solely for local app functionality:

* **Haptic Feedback / Vibration:** Used solely to deliver tactile vibration when pressing buttons, toggling options, or when an event countdown celebration triggers.
* **Internet Access:** Used solely when you explicitly tap external links, such as opening the Google Play Store to rate the app, or contacting support via your chosen email client.
* **System Share Sheet:** Used solely when you explicitly tap the **Share** button on a countdown card to format and share a text summary with friends or external apps via your device's native share menu.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services & Integrations

The App utilizes minimal standard platform libraries to support native operating system functionality:

* **Google Play Core / In-App Review:** Used to open the Google Play Store listing when you choose to rate the app. This is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Email Client (`mailto:`):** When you tap **Contact Us**, the App invokes your device's default email client with a prefilled template addressed to `dr.usman7860@gmail.com`. No message content is collected by Remaini.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Remaini does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because the App does not collect any personal data from any user, it is entirely safe for users of all ages.

---

## 7. Data Retention & Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under international data protection regulations (such as GDPR in Europe and CCPA/CPRA in California):

* **Access & Portability:** You have complete, direct access to all your countdown data stored within the app on your physical device.
* **Erasure & Deletion:** You can delete individual events at any time by swiping left or using the delete button. You can also wipe all application data instantly by tapping **Settings ➔ Reset All Events**, clearing the app data in Android/iOS Settings, or uninstalling the App.

---

## 8. Security of Your Information

Because all your data remains exclusively on your personal device, the security of your countdown information relies on your device’s security settings (e.g., screen lock, biometrics, and device encryption). We recommend keeping your device operating system updated.
