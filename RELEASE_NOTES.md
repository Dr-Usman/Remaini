# Remaini v1.0.0 — Initial Official Release ⏳🚀

We are thrilled to introduce **Remaini**, a modern, minimal, and offline-first countdown application built with Flutter.

---

### ✨ What's New in v1.0.0:

#### 🏠 Smart Event Feed & Spotlight
- **Hero Spotlight Card**: Automatically showcases your nearest upcoming event.
- **Dynamic Urgency Accents**: Color-coded badges for *Far Away*, *Approaching*, *Soon*, *Today*, and *Completed*.
- **Search, Filter & Sort**: Filter by 8 categories (*Personal, Work, Birthday, Holiday, Travel, Milestone, etc.*) and sort dynamically.
- **Swipe Gestures**: Swipe right to pin/unpin events, swipe left to delete with instant 1-tap undo.

#### ⏱️ Precision Live Countdown Showcase
- **Live 1-Second Ticker**: Continuous down-to-the-second countdown stream with micro-slide digit animations.
- **Glowing Neon Circular Ring**: Custom-painted progress arc displaying elapsed ratio vs. event lifespan.
- **Dual Breakdown Modes**: Seamless toggle between *Calendar Units* (*Weeks, Days, Hours, Mins, Secs*) and *Cumulative Totals* (*Total Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds*).
- **🎉 Milestone Celebration**: Interactive celebration screen when a countdown reaches zero.
- **Social Sharing**: One-tap formatted summary to share events with friends and family.

#### ➕ Frictionless Creation Flow
- **1-Tap Quick Date Shortcuts**: `Tomorrow`, `Weekend`, `+1 Week`, `+1 Month`, `New Year`.
- **1-Tap Quick Time Shortcuts**: `9:00 AM`, `12:00 PM`, `6:00 PM`, `Midnight`.
- **Customization**: 8 built-in category icons, 10 vibrant accent swatches, and memo notes.

#### ⚙️ Polish, Themes & Privacy
- **Adaptive Appearance**: Seamless support for System, Dark, and Light themes.
- **Tactile Haptics**: Subtle haptic feedback across buttons, swipes, and view switches.
- **100% Offline & Private**: Zero analytics, zero ads, zero data tracking. Powered by Hive CE local storage.
